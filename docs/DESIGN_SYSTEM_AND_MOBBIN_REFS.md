## 1. Visual Direction: "The Conductor's Observatory"
A deliberate departure from generic AI dark-mode gradients and identical SaaS cards. Combines two high-contrast environments:
- **Observatory Dark (`#070A0F` Carbon Obsidian / `#0E141E` Deck / `#121A27` Slate):** Hero spatial constellation, Flagship Ventures, Exvorta Web Trade Houses, Autonomous Agent Pipeline, Monumental Footer.
- **Architectural Drafting Folio (`#EDF1F5` Daylight Paper / `#0B1320` Deep Ink):** Chapter 04 Career Ledger, Multi-Track Resume Switcher, and Capabilities Matrix, separated by `1px` architectural hairlines and `+` registration crosshairs.
- **Dual Signal Accents:** Firoza Turquoise (`#12B896`) for live telemetry & active pipeline gates + Exvorta Sovereign Brass (`#C9A227`) for edition stamps and verified highlights.

## 2. Typography & Bilingual RTL (`EN` / `FA`)
- **English Display:** `Instrument Serif` (Regular + Italic) paired with `Syne` (Bold/ExtraBold geometric architectural display).
- **English Body:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`400, 500, 600, 700`).
- **Telemetry / Code:** `JetBrains Mono` (`400, 500`).
- **Persian (`FA` RTL):** `Vazirmatn` variable weights (`400..900`), preventing synthetic bold, paired with automatic Persian numeral formatting (`toPersianDigits`) and full directional mirroring (`dir="rtl"`).
