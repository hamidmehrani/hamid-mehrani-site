  - `Nishapur Verdigris` (`#0EC49A`) — Live telemetry, verified gates, spatial vectors

  - `Drafting Vellum` (`#EEF1F5` + `#0A111C` Carbon Ink) — High-contrast Daylight Architectural Folio for `/resume`, `/hire`, and proof ledgers
- **Typography:**
  - **EN Display & Numerals:** `Instrument Serif` (Regular & Italic) + tight-tracked `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`-0.035em`). Dropped `Syne` to eliminate squat wide-sans clichés; avoided single-word colored/italicized headline gimmicks.
